FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions and short answers.

How often is the pool water checked?

During the season pH and chlorine are measured at every visit; frequency depends on the pool type.

Do you do winterising and season opening?

Yes; draining equipment, cover fitting and spring start-up are offered.

Are the images and data on the site real?

No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.
